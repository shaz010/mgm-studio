Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Your Salon Pro ("the App") is operated by Shahbaz Mirshahi as a solo independent developer. This policy explains how the App handles your information.

Data Collection

Your Salon Pro does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with third parties. All data you enter into the App — appointments, client names, earnings, commission rates, and settings — is stored locally on your device using your browser's local storage.

Data Storage

  • All app data is stored locally on your device.
  • No data is sent to any external server by default.
  • Optional Firebase sync (if enabled) stores your data securely in Google Firebase under your account. Firebase's own privacy policy applies in this case.

Analytics

The App does not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.

Cookies

The App uses browser local storage to save your preferences and app data. No third-party cookies are used.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for professional use by adults. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The updated date at the top of this page reflects the most recent version.
